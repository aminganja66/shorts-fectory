# Research: Zomato started as menu-card scanning

## Facts (conservative values)
1. Origin: Goyal and Chaddah were Bain & Company colleagues in Delhi. The office cafe kept about 50 restaurant menu cards, stapled together. They scanned the cards and put them on an intranet site for Bain staff. Traffic convinced them it could be a business. Source: https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/article/menu-and-restaurant-listing-sites-search-for-scale-in-india/
2. Launch: Foodiebay is dated to 2008 (July 2008 in several accounts). Exact day varies by source, so say only "2008". Goyal reportedly kept scanning and uploading menus on weekends while still at Bain, for about 1.5 years (his own account, reported). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eternal_Limited
3. Rename: Foodiebay became Zomato in 2010 (YourStory dated it Nov 2010). Wikipedia says 2012 for the legal name Zomato Media, so say "2010 rebrand" and avoid the legal-name claim. Reason (Mint 2015, via Wikipedia): avoid a potential trademark conflict with eBay and prepare to go beyond food. Sources: https://yourstory.com/2010/11/foodiebay-rebranded-as-zomato-com-plans-to-enter-nightlife-business/amp ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eternal_Limited
4. Funding: first investor Info Edge (Naukri.com), US$1 million in July 2010 (about Rs 4.7 crore, reported; some sources say Dec 2010), then US$5.5 million in Aug 2011. Source: https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/article/menu-and-restaurant-listing-sites-search-for-scale-in-india/
5. IPO: July 2021, Rs 9,375 crore raised (Rs 9,000 crore fresh + Rs 375 crore OFS), price Rs 76, subscribed about 38 times. Listed 23 July 2021 about 52% above issue price on NSE, closed about 66% up. Market cap crossed Rs 1 lakh crore (about Rs 98,700 crore at close; conservative). Source: https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/companies/zomato-shares-zoom-66-on-listing-day-settle-at-126-apiece-at-closing-bell-121072301212_1.html
6. Latest: Eternal (Zomato's parent, renamed 2025) Q1 results 22 Jul 2026: revenue Rs 20,211 crore (up 182%), net profit Rs 92 crore, market cap about Rs 2.79 lakh crore that day. Source: https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/markets/stocks/story/eternal-q1-earnings-net-profit-rises-268-revenue-at-rs-20211-crore-544490-2026-07-22
7. Earlier: Q2 FY26 (Oct 2025) revenue Rs 13,590 crore, profit Rs 65 crore. Source: https://www.business-standard.com/amp/companies/quarterly-results/eternal-q2-results-profit-65-cr-revenue-doubles-quick-commerce-growth-125101600845_1.html

## Twist
A free fix for a lunch-queue annoyance (about 50 stapled menus) became a company listed at over Rs 1 lakh crore intraday in 2021. Hook idea: stapled paper menus -> a Rs 1 lakh crore company.

## Caveats
- Launch date (Jan vs Jul 2008), rename year (2010 vs 2012 legal), and Info Edge date conflict across sources. Use year-only.
- "50 menu cards" comes from one Wharton summary; say "about 50" or "reportedly".
- Mint/ET/Fortune pages were not retrievable; the scanning story is mostly Goyal's own account. Mark as "reportedly" in script.
