Facts & sources

  • Deepinder Goyal and Pankaj Chaddah were colleagues at Bain & Company in Delhi. The office cafe reportedly kept about 50 restaurant menu cards stapled together; they scanned them and put them on an intranet site for staff, and the traffic convinced them it could be a business. — knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu
  • Foodiebay launched in 2008 (exact day varies by source). — en.wikipedia.org
  • Foodiebay was renamed Zomato in 2010; reportedly (Mint, 2015, via Wikipedia) to avoid a potential trademark conflict with eBay and to go beyond food. — yourstory.com, en.wikipedia.org
  • Info Edge (Naukri.com) invested US$1 million in 2010, its first outside funding (some sources say July, some December). — knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu
  • Zomato's IPO in July 2021 raised Rs 9,375 crore, was subscribed about 38 times, listed 23 July 2021 and closed about 66% above the issue price on day one. — www.business-standard.com
