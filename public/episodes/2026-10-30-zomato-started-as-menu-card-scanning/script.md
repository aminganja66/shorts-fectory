## Facts & sources
- Deepinder Goyal and Pankaj Chaddah were colleagues at Bain & Company in Delhi. The office cafe reportedly kept about 50 restaurant menu cards stapled together; they scanned them and put them on an intranet site for staff, and the traffic convinced them it could be a business. — https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/article/menu-and-restaurant-listing-sites-search-for-scale-in-india/
- Foodiebay launched in 2008 (exact day varies by source). — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eternal_Limited
- Foodiebay was renamed Zomato in 2010; reportedly (Mint, 2015, via Wikipedia) to avoid a potential trademark conflict with eBay and to go beyond food. — https://yourstory.com/2010/11/foodiebay-rebranded-as-zomato-com-plans-to-enter-nightlife-business/amp, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eternal_Limited
- Info Edge (Naukri.com) invested US$1 million in 2010, its first outside funding (some sources say July, some December). — https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/article/menu-and-restaurant-listing-sites-search-for-scale-in-india/
- Zomato's IPO in July 2021 raised Rs 9,375 crore, was subscribed about 38 times, listed 23 July 2021 and closed about 66% above the issue price on day one. — https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/companies/zomato-shares-zoom-66-on-listing-day-settle-at-126-apiece-at-closing-bell-121072301212_1.html
