# Research: Airbnb founders paid the rent by selling cereal boxes

Checked 2026-10-28. Every fact in storyboard.json → facts[] has a source URL.

## Framing note (important for the script)
- The **rent** part is the **2007** air-mattress story (Fortune 2015: broke, with rent due, October 2007).
- The **cereal** came in **late 2008**. It paid down the founders' **credit card debt** and kept the company running. It did not pay the rent.
- Accurate framing: "they were broke, behind on credit cards, and kept the company alive by selling cereal." Don't say the cereal paid the rent.

## Founding details (sourced only)
- Place: San Francisco apartment (Fortune 2015; Airbnb About us).
- Year: 2007 (3 guests). Website: August 2008.
- First product: air mattresses on the floor during a design trade show.
- First money for the company: cereal sales (about $30,000 per Chesky), then YC's $20,000 for 6%.

## Conflicts resolved
- **Box price:** TechCrunch (2008) says $39. Most later accounts (Gebbia via Fred Wilson, Graham via Chesky) say $40. Script: "$40" or "about $40". Both are sourced.
- **Box count:** 500 of each = 1,000 made (TechCrunch; Gebbia). CNBC says "sold 1,000", but Gebbia says Cap'n McCain's sold poorly, so not all 1,000 sold. Script: "they made 1,000 boxes" or "500 of each". Don't say "sold 1,000".
- **Money raised:** Chesky says about $30,000 (Fortune 2023). CNBC says $40,000. Wikipedia says $20–30k. Use **about $30,000**.
- **Credit card debt:** Chesky said $25,000 (Masters of Scale 2017, via CNBC). Gebbia: "tens of thousands". Other blogs give $20k or $40k each. Use **$25,000 (Chesky)**.
- **YC year:** Fortune says the $20,000 deal was in 2008. The class was Winter 2009. Script: "Y Combinator" without a year, or "early 2009".

## Quotes: what is safe
- SAFE (Chesky's memory, reported by Fortune): Graham said that if they could get people to pay $40 for $4 boxes of cereal, "maybe, just maybe, you can convince strangers..." Paraphrase it, or quote only the part up to "convince strangers", and say "Chesky says Graham told them".
- SAFE: Chesky's 2020 tweet, quoted by CNBC: "We funded Airbnb by selling these."
- SAFE: Chesky's January 2009 email: "This week so far we have done $734 in fees."
- NOT SAFE: The **"cockroach"** line ("you are cockroaches, you will not die"). It only appears on blogs that say they're quoting a NYT article, and I couldn't find a reliable primary source. Don't quote it. A safe wording: "Graham liked founders who wouldn't die."
