# Research: Jio gave India free data and broke the telecom market

## Verified facts (safe for script)
1. **Launch + free offer.** Jio's commercial service started **September 5, 2016**. The "Welcome Offer" made voice, data and SMS free until **December 31, 2016** (Ambani announced it at the RIL AGM on Sept 1, 2016). TRAI later capped new sign-ups for the offer at Dec 3, 2016.
   - https://www.businesstoday.in/industry/telecom/story/all-about-free-welcome-offer-from-mukesh-ambani-s-reliance-jio-145941-2016-09-01
   - https://canalys.com/resources/Jio-launch-a-turning-point-in-the-Indian-market
2. **Growth.** **100 million subscribers in 170 days**, about **7 new subscribers per second** (Jio media release, Feb 21, 2017). "Faster than Facebook, WhatsApp, Skype" is Jio's own claim.
   - https://www.ril.com/sites/default/files/2023-01/Media-Release-JIO-21022017.pdf
3. **Data price.** TRAI data: **Rs 269/GB (2014) to Rs 11.78/GB (2018), about 95% lower**; Rs 75.57 (2016), Rs 19.35 (2017). Govt in Parliament (Dec 2024): Rs 8.31/GB in June 2024. Use "about Rs 269 to about Rs 12" (conservative). Note: the drop is the whole market's average, not only Jio's price.
   - https://www.business-standard.com/article/pti-stories/mobile-data-price-down-by-95-but-revenue-up-2-5-times-in-5-yrs-trai-119082101291_1.html
   - https://www.newsonair.gov.in/cost-of-data-drastically-reduced-from-%e2%82%b9269-gb-in-march-2014-to-%e2%82%b98-31-gb-in-june-2024-govt
4. **Investment (reported, company's own figure).** Ambani, March 30, 2016 (FICCI Frames): Reliance had invested **Rs 1.5 lakh crore** in Jio. Not from a 2016 AGM. Later totals grew (about Rs 1.9 lakh crore reported in 2017), so say "more than Rs 1.5 lakh crore before launch, by Ambani's own count".
   - https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/corporate/story/reliance-jio-biggest-startup-investment-mukesh-ambani-57894-2016-03-30
5. **Rival fallout.** Aircel filed for insolvency at the NCLT on **Feb 28, 2018**, blaming "intense competition after the disruptive entry of a new player", debt and losses. Airtel completed its takeover of **Telenor India in May 2018**. Vodafone India + Idea completed their merger on **Aug 31, 2018** (about 408M customers).
   - https://www.businesstoday.in/sectors/telecom/aircel-files-for-bankruptcy-insolvency-reliance-jio-entry/story/271674.html
   - https://www.mobileworldlive.com/asia-pacific/airtel-completes-acquisition-of-telenor-india/
   - https://www.telecomtv.com/content/vodafone/vodafone-announces-the-completion-of-the-merger-between-vodafone-india-and-idea-32198
6. **Today.** **533.3 million** subscribers as of June 30, 2026 (Q1 FY27). Q2 FY27 numbers were not found yet. Script: "over 500 million".
   - https://telecomtalk.info/jio-adds-million-subscribers-q1fy27-arpu-rs215/1009790/
   - https://indiantelevision.com/mam/jio-platforms-q1-profit-rises-9-2-per-cent-as-subscriber-base-tops-533-million/

## Use with care / avoid
- **"India went from 155th to No. 1 in mobile data"**: this is Ambani's claim (IMC Oct 2018), and it mixes metrics (broadband *penetration* rank vs data *consumption*). He also said 150th in 2017. If used, say "according to Ambani". Jio's 2017 release claims India became "the leading country in the world for mobile data usage". https://www.digit.in/news/general/india-will-be-a-fully-4g-country-by-2020-and-ready-for-5g-ahead-of-others-mukesh-ambani-at-india-mob-44313.html
- Don't say Jio "killed" Aircel or Telenor. Say Aircel blamed "a disruptive new player" and Telenor sold to Airtel. The Vodafone–Idea merger was reported as a response to Jio's pressure.
- AGR dues were left out because they make the story longer and more legally complicated.
- Reuters coverage wasn't reachable in search, so trade/business outlets are used instead. Business Standard and some PDFs blocked direct fetch, so their figures were cross-checked against multiple reports.
- Not investment advice: no share price, IPO or valuation talk.
