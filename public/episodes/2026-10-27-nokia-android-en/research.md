# Research: Nokia ने Android को ना कहा

## Facts (safe to script)
1. Sept 3, 2013: Microsoft agreed to buy Nokia's Devices & Services business for EUR 3.79bn plus EUR 1.65bn for patent licence = EUR 5.44bn total in cash. Close expected Q1 2014. (One secondary site dates it Sept 2; official releases say Sept 3.)
   - https://news.microsoft.com/source/2013/09/03/microsoft-to-acquire-nokias-devices-services-business-license-nokias-patents-and-mapping-services/
2. Feb 2011: Elop's "burning platform" memo (North Sea oil platform analogy) leaked/published Feb 8, 2011, confirmed real; Nokia announced Windows Phone as its primary smartphone platform on Feb 11, 2011 (Capital Markets Day), and the share price fell sharply. Reuters link not found; sources are Engadget/TechCrunch/Communicate.
   - https://www.engadget.com/2011/02/08/nokia-ceo-stephen-elop-rallies-troops-in-brutally-honest-burnin/
   - https://techcrunch.com/2011/02/07/nokia-heading-to-silicon-valley-and-the-standing-on-a-burning-platform-memo-2
3. Why not Android (Elop's own stated reasoning, reported): worry about a "collision" with Samsung, who already dominated Android, and difficulty differentiating late. Elop said Nokia explored Android with Google. Say "Elop ने बाद में कहा / रिपोर्ट्स के मुताबिक".
   - https://www.computing.co.uk/news/2341940/nokia-picked-windows-phone-over-android-to-avoid-samsung-collision
   - https://www.techradar.com/news/phone-and-communications/mobile-phones/elop-admits-that-nokia-was-scared-to-go-head-to-head-with-samsung-1245745
4. Gartner smartphone share: Nokia 49.4% in 2007, 43.7% in 2008 (smartphones, not all phones). Symbian: 62.3% of smartphone OS in 2007 to 47.1% in Q4 2008; 22.1% in Q2 2011 (from 40.9% a year earlier).
   - https://www.fierce-network.com/developer/smartphone-sales-increase-13-9-2008
   - https://www.allaboutsymbian.com/news/item/13181_Worldwide_Q2_2011_stats_out_fr.php
5. Gartner full-year 2011 (all phones): Nokia 422m units, 23.8% share (28.9% in 2010); Samsung 314m, 17.7%. Samsung passed Nokia in SMARTPHONES in Q3 2011 (about 24-25m vs 19.5m), per Gartner. Nokia stayed #1 overall phone maker in 2011 per these numbers; a May 2012 headline claiming Samsung "dethroned" Nokia refers to different counting -> do NOT say Nokia lost the #1 handset spot in 2011.
   - https://www.businesstoday.in/story/nokia-loses-top-handset-maker-position-to-samsung-gartner/1/184596.html (conflicting, treat carefully)

## Disagreements / do not claim
- "Nokia had 40% of all phones at peak": not verified; 40%+ figures found are for smartphones. Q1 2009 total phone share 36.2% (Gartner, per mobilenewscwp). Use smartphone numbers above.
- Microsoft platform-support payments to Nokia: not verified. Do not claim.
- Nokia employees to Microsoft: ~32,000 reported (4,700 in Finland), reported only.
- Not verified: any Android prototype/internal Android project; only Elop's statements.

## Twist for script
Nokia said no to Android, picked Windows Phone in Feb 2011, and 2.5 years later sold the phone business to Microsoft for EUR 5.44bn (Sept 2013).
