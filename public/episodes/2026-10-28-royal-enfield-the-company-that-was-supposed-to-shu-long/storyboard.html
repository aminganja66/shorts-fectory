Angle

A motorcycle brand reportedly slated for closure in 2000 is now selling over a million bikes a year.

Facts & sources

  • In 2000, when Siddhartha Lal took charge, the Royal Enfield division was reportedly making large losses; Eicher chairman S. Sandilya said it was "slated for closure". — www.outlookbusiness.com
  • Eicher sold 1,227,977 Royal Enfield motorcycles in FY26 (results 22 May 2026), up 22%. — www.autocarpro.in
  • The Eicher Group acquired Enfield India in 1994 and renamed it Royal Enfield Motors Limited. — www.royalenfield.com
  • 1891: Bob Walker Smith and Albert Eadie bought George Townsend & Co. in Redditch; in 1893 they won a contract for parts for the Royal Small Arms Factory at Enfield and renamed the business Enfield Manufacturing Company. — www.royalenfield.com
  • 1901: the first Royal Enfield motorcycle was launched at the Stanley Cycle Show in London for £50 with a 1¾ hp engine. — www.royalenfield.com
  • 1932: the Bullet was introduced. — www.royalenfield.com
  • 1949: K. R. Sundaram Iyer founded Madras Motors to import British bikes; in the early 1950s the Indian Army ordered Bullets for border patrol (order size disputed: 500 or 800). — www.royalenfield.com, theprint.in
  • 1955: Enfield India was formed with Madras Motors, assembling Bullets from Redditch kits; the Tiruvottiyur factory started in 1956 and built 163 Bullets that year. — theprint.in
  • 1962: the first fully made-in-India Enfield was built. — theprint.in
  • The British company was gone by about 1970-71 (Redditch factory closed 1967); Enfield India continued and later got the Royal Enfield name rights. — en.wikipedia.org
  • Hero Honda was formed in 1984; its 100cc CD100 started production in 1985 at Dharuhera. — global.honda
  • Around 2000, Bullet sales were reportedly below 2,000 a month; Lal later said growth went "from 2,000 per month to over 10,000 per month". — www.autocarpro.in, www.outlookindia.com
  • Lal took charge in 2000, aged about 26; sales were about 25,000 units in 2004; in 2005 Eicher sold 13 of its 15 divisions to focus on two-wheelers. — www.outlookbusiness.com
  • The Classic 350 with the new UCE engine launched in 2009; volumes grew about 22% a year from 2010 to 2018. — www.outlookbusiness.com
  • The 648cc Interceptor 650 and Continental GT 650 were unveiled at EICMA Milan on 7 Nov 2017 and went on sale in India in Nov 2018. — businessworld.in
  • FY25: 1,002,893 units (902,757 domestic, 100,136 exports), the first year above one million; Eicher revenue Rs 18,870 crore, profit Rs 4,734 crore. — www.business-standard.com
  • FY26: 1,227,977 units (1,107,343 domestic, 120,634 exports); Eicher revenue Rs 23,408 crore, profit Rs 5,515 crore. — www.autocarpro.in
  • Eicher announced Rs 958 crore for the Cheyyar plant near Chennai, lifting capacity from 1.46 million to 2 million units a year (reported). — www.constructionworld.in
  • By 1977 Enfield India reportedly had no reserves after losses and a long strike; losses cleared by 1981; by 1987 a new sales low against Japanese 100cc bikes; Eicher took a stake around 1990 and full ownership about three years later. — madrasmusings.com
  • October 2000: Eicher posted its first quarterly loss in 10 years; Royal Enfield was about one-fifth of revenue. — archives.digitaltoday.in
  • Riders complained of engine seizures, snapping cables, electrical failures and oil leaks; Lal, 26, reportedly argued for one more chance when the board weighed closure or sale. — www.businesstoday.in
  • Quality push: a "back to basics" 2006; sprag clutch failures fell from 5% (2005/06) to 0.2% (2010/11); new aluminium engine with 30% fewer parts and 30% more power; gear lever moved left; Classic 350 launched Nov 2009 at about Rs 1.20 lakh. — www.businesstoday.in
  • About a decade before 2012 the Enfield 350 cost about Rs 55,000 on-road and broke down often; monthly volume went from about 2,000 to over 10,000. — www.autocarpro.in
  • 2011 sales about 75,000, 2012 about 113,000; the Rs 150-crore Oragadam plant opened on 30 April 2013. — www.autocarpro.in
  • The Himalayan launched in March 2016; the UK Technology Centre at Bruntingthorpe, Leicestershire, was inaugurated on 6 Nov 2017 (120+ staff). — www.autocarpro.in, www.business-standard.com
  • FY20 sales fell 16% to 695,947 (from 826,098 in FY19) and production stopped from 23 March 2020 (COVID); FY22: 602,268; FY23: record 834,895 (+~38%), exports above 1 lakh for the first time. — www.businesstoday.in
  • The Manaus (Brazil) assembly plant opened on 8 Nov 2022. — autocarpro.in
  • On the Pali–Jodhpur highway in Rajasthan, travellers reportedly pray at a roadside shrine to a Bullet (local legend). — www.aljazeera.com
