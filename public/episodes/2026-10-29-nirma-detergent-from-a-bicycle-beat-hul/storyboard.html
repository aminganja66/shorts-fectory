Facts & sources

  • Karsanbhai Patel started selling homemade detergent door to door in Ahmedabad in 1969. — en.wikipedia.org, www.retail4growth.com
  • Before that he worked as a chemist in the Gujarat government's Department of Mining and Geology (some sources say lab technician/assistant). — en.wikipedia.org, www.thebetterindia.com
  • Nirma sold at about Rs 3 per kg while HUL's Surf cost roughly Rs 13 (sources vary: Rs 3 to 3.50 vs Rs 10 to 15). Use 'about 3 rupees vs about 13'. — en.wikipedia.org, www.retail4growth.com
  • The name Nirma comes from his daughter Nirupama (her death in an accident is reported only by The Better India / IMPACT). — www.thebetterindia.com, www.retail4growth.com
  • Nirma reportedly passed Surf as India's top detergent around 1985, with an estimated share of about 60% (reported, secondary sources). — www.retail4growth.com, www.storyboard18.com
  • HUL responded by launching the low-priced Wheel detergent, reportedly in 1988 (single-source date; 'Operation STING'). — www.retail4growth.com
  • By 2014 Nirma's detergent share was about 10% among roughly 400 brands (Business Standard, market estimates). — www.retail4growth.com
  • The Nirma group later moved into cement (Lafarge India for $1.4 billion in 2016) and in Sept 2023 bought 75% of Glenmark Life Sciences for Rs 5,652 crore (about $680 million). — en.wikipedia.org
