# Research: Nirma vs HUL Surf (brand, Short)

## Facts (safe wording for script)
1. Karsanbhai Patel began selling a homemade detergent door to door in Ahmedabad in 1969. Sources: Wikipedia Karsanbhai_Patel (cites Times of India/The Hindu/GQ India); retail4growth (syndicated Business Standard, Nov 2014). 
2. He was a chemist with the Gujarat government's Department of Mining and Geology (Wikipedia Nirma; The Better India). Some sources say "lab technician/assistant" -> say "a government chemist".
3. Price: Nirma Rs 3 per kg (Business Standard 2014, conservative) vs Surf about Rs 13-15 (Wikipedia: Rs 3.50 vs Rs 13; Better India: Rs 3 vs Rs 10-15). Conservative: "about Rs 3 a kilo vs roughly Rs 13 for Surf" or "a fraction of Surf's price" (BS: at peak avg Rs 14 below Surf, reported). DON'T say exactly 3.50 vs 15.
4. Name: from his daughter Nirupama (Better India, Wikipedia, BS-syndicated). That she died in an accident is reported by Better India/IMPACT only; Wikipedia does not say it. Keep out of script or say "reportedly".
5. Started with ~15-20 packets a day, made in a small room at home; he cycled ~15 km to his office (Wikipedia Nirma, unverified primary) - REPORTED only.
6. Nirma overtook Surf and became India's top detergent around 1985 (BS-syndicated, "reportedly"; Storyboard18 also says 1985). Share ~60% of the market (reported, ~1.72 lakh tonnes; secondary sources). Conservative: "more than half" flagged reported.
7. HUL's response: launched Wheel, a low-priced detergent, 1988 as "Operation STING" (retail4growth/BS-syndicated; ICMR case describes HLL first dismissive then moving to low-income segment). Single-source date; Storyboard18 implies ~1985-ish. Say "later launched Wheel" without year unless confirmed; "1988 (reported)" if year needed.
8. Surf reportedly avoided cutting price for fear of diluting brand equity (BS-syndicated; reported).
9. Today: Nirma's detergent share ~10% in 2014 (BS, market estimates). Wheel later topped the market in early 2000s, then Ghari overtook both (BS-syndicated; ICMR/case says Ghari passed Wheel in 2011). Nirma group moved into cement/soda ash: Searles Valley 2007, Lafarge India cement $1.4bn 2016 (Reuters/The Hindu), Emami Cement Rs 5,500 cr 2020, Glenmark Life Sciences 75% Rs 5,652 cr Sept 2023 (Reuters). Forbes Oct 2024: ~$6.05bn net worth, rank 48 (Wikipedia citing Forbes; Hurun $9bn differs, use Forbes/conservative).
10. Padma Shri 2010 (Ministry of Home Affairs, via Wikipedia).
